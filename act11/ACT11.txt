[PART 7: Focus Questions]
Answer briefly:

1. Why do some commands require sudo?
Commands like tcpdump require sudo because they need root privileges to put the network card into promiscuous mode and capture raw packets directly from the hardware interface.

2. What is the importance of nmap in cybersecurity?
It allows to discover active hosts on a network, identify open ports, and determine what services are running.

3. How can tcpdump help in monitoring attacks?
It captures real-time packet data traversing the network.
